My Role: Designing SOPs

Translating our bespoke furniture experience into automated rules for our agents

Guiding Our Virtual Staff Step-by-Step

Virtual agents are highly competent but require a playbook. I design the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to ensure every agent follows our business objectives flawlessly, preventing any AI errors.

Keeping our operations secure, consistent, and fully transparent.

INTAKE SOP
Extract & Organize Requirements

Instructs Intake Agent to parse client portal chats and automatically draft bilingual BOMs with precise items and quantities.

SAFETY SOP
British Fire Code Hard Gate

Instructs Safety Agent to immediately block non-compliant fabrics (e.g. Pure Silk) and alert us if Crib 5 certification is missing.

FINANCIAL SOP
Strike-through Recalculation

Instructs Auditor Agent to instantly recalculate and cross-out canceled items in the payment schedules in case of mid-production changes.

Why This Plan Protects Us

Smart business safeguards and operational safety
Zero Ban Risk

No WhatsApp Bans

By shifting core client chats to our secure internal Portal, we completely bypass Meta's strict automated anti-spam number bans. WhatsApp is used only for outbox notifications.

Identity Lock

Strict Data Isolation

Because clients must register and log into our portal, every action is locked to their secure ID, completely preventing order crosstalk or database leaks.

High Margin

Save Transaction Fees

Using our custom native web-sockets for core customer intake rather than third-party WhatsApp API meters saves us thousands of dollars in bulk messaging fees.
